06 / 12

Write-back

The system of record must not receive a cleaned-up claim we invented. If the destination changed under us, we do not overwrite. We put a drift card on the queue. A person decides.

May write, gated

  • Copy of inbound codes, units, dollars, date, payer

    From the inbound snapshot, the extract, or the 835.

  • Copy of 835 CARC / RARC / ICN

    From remit bytes. Never a guessed allowed amount.

  • Overlay status as a new task or flag

    Citing the link. Denial category, AR band, appeal draft still unsent.

Must not write

  • Recoded CPT or ICD

    Coding is out of v1.

  • Guessed allowed amounts

    Park. Never infer allowed.

  • Overwrite of clinical codes

    Overlay opinions are not chart edits.

  • Silent overwrite on drift

    A person decides.

Out of v1

A later operating-system design exists: nine agents, a full claim machine, outbound file, voice. It is recorded so it is not rediscovered as the first module. v1 is the overlay. AlfredCare’s own charting and coding stay on their platform. They are not this overlay.

Later — after overlay and one book

  • Coding agent

    Only after a cited appeal a person actually sent.

  • Outbound 837 / auto-file

    We do not submit claims in v1. Payer hop waits on the 835.

  • Voice / payer call

    Specified with zero state authority. Not v1.

  • Eligibility, auth, posting graphs

    Named later. Posting is append-only.

Out of this overlay

  • Patient or member app

    The queue is internal.

  • Auto-send, auto-refund, auto-remediate

    The overlay does not mail, file, or remediate.

  • A chatbot wrap

    Staff work exceptions. They do not chat with a model.

  • Buying a GPU floor

    API-first. No owned hardware in v1.

11 / 12

Proof

Empty is better than a demo that files, or a demo that overwrites an EHR. The method is proven. The overlay is not yet on a live book.

What would count as working

  • Denials classified with CARC/RARC extracted from 835 bytes, not guessed.
  • AR queue ranked; dead-clock tickets refuse; park does not enter cite-or-refuse.
  • A cited appeal draft against the policy version in force on the date of service, unsent, a named person on it.
  • Three hops on every claim card. Missing source refuses ingest.
  • Write-back of proven fields only. Drift is a card, not an overwrite.
  • Shadow compared to what the billing company actually did, then operator promotion.

Certainty today

  • The retrieve-to-refuse loop is proven on synthetic test files.
  • Policy index and contracts are specified and in review.
  • Denials, AR, appeals, write-back, and surfaces are specified, not started.
  • No live book. No BAA in the product repository. AlfredCare is a partner — GTM contract in counsel, not signed.
  • No take-rate is printed as a price.
  • No first-pass rate is printed as a compliance number.
